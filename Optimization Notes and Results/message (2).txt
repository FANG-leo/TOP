优化原因（死锁溯源）：
    在第十二部分 (Part 12) 的测试中发现，多进程 (np > 1) 运行会发生死锁。对底层通信路径的排查揭示了 lbm_comm_halo_exchange(...) 函数中存在的两个致命缺陷：

        荒谬的强制休眠：边界交换末尾调用的一个自定义 MPI_Syncall(...) 函数，实际上触发了一段生成的存根代码，该代码在多进程路径的每个时间步里硬生生地插入了 1 秒钟的睡眠（Sleep）延迟。

        极度破碎的通信流：旧的边界交换逻辑使用了大量零碎的阻塞式发送/接收（MPI_Send / MPI_Recv），并在其间穿插了反复的全局屏障（MPI_Barrier）同步，导致多进程通信极度脆弱且低效。

    优化过程：
    为了根除死锁并提升通信效率，对边界交换逻辑进行了彻底的批量化重写：

        移除休眠延迟：彻底删除了自定义的 MPI_Syncall(...) 包装路径，移除了每步一秒的隐形惩罚。

        引入连续缓冲区打包：废弃了零碎的收发辅助函数，新增了针对边界列、边界行和角落网格的打包/解包（Packing/Unpacking）机制，将数据集中到连续的缓冲区中。

        使用 MPI_Sendrecv 替换阻塞屏障：基于批量打包的数据，使用更高效安全的 MPI_Sendrecv(...) 重新编排了上、下、左、右及四个角的交换流程，完全移除了旧代码中冗长的阻塞消息和全局 MPI_Barrier。

    优化结果与关键突破：

        彻底修复死锁：多进程路径完全恢复正常，不再发生阻塞挂起。

        性能狂飙：在两个 MPI 进程（np=2, omp=1）的配置下运行 20,000 次迭代，耗时大幅降至 20.27 秒，品质因数 (FOM) 狂飙至 256.85 MLUPS。

        重大意义：这是项目自开始优化以来，第一次在性能上通过并行（多进程）手段取得实质性的巨大飞跃。它不仅打破了之前单进程串行 109.62 MLUPS 的极限，更重要的是，扫清了底层通信障碍，为接下来进行更大规模的 MPI/OpenMP 扩展测试重新铺平了道路。

English Version

    Reason for Optimization (Deadlock Root-Cause):
    Following the parallel baseline test in Part 12, which revealed that multi-rank runs (np > 1) were stalling, an inspection of the communication path (lbm_comm_halo_exchange(...)) exposed two critical flaws:

        Artificial Sleep Penalty: A custom MPI_Syncall(...) call at the end of the halo exchange triggered a generated sync stub that effectively inserted a massive one-second sleep per timestep on the multi-rank path.

        Fragmented Communication: The halo exchange relied on many small, blocking MPI_Send / MPI_Recv operations separated by repeated global MPI_Barrier(...) calls, making the execution extremely fragile and artificially slow.

    Optimization Process:
    The fragmented halo exchange was entirely replaced with a simpler, safer bulk-exchange scheme:

        Eliminating Artificial Delays: The custom MPI_Syncall(...) tail call and its generated sync-wrapper path were completely removed, eliminating the hidden per-step sleep.

        Bulk Packing into Contiguous Buffers: Replaced the fragmented horizontal, vertical, and diagonal ghost exchange helpers with new internal mechanisms to pack/unpack boundary columns, rows, and corner cells into contiguous buffers.

        Replacing Barriers with MPI_Sendrecv: The entire halo exchange was rewritten to use MPI_Sendrecv(...) for swapping opposite faces and corners. This eliminated the long, fragile sequence of blocking pointwise sends and global barriers.

    Optimization Results & Critical Breakthrough:

        Deadlock Resolved: The multi-rank MPI path was fully recovered and now completes reliably without stalling.

        Massive Performance Surge: Running the 20,000-iteration benchmark across two MPI ranks (np=2, omp=1) dropped the elapsed time to just 20.27 seconds and skyrocketed the FOM to 256.85 MLUPS.

        Significance: This marks the first time parallelization (multi-processing) successfully delivered a massive performance leap. Not only did it shatter the previous single-rank serial ceiling of 109.62 MLUPS, but it also successfully reopened the door for the larger MPI/OpenMP scaling study that had been previously blocked.
